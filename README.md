Exactly one emoji — the one most likely to make a stranger smile — for the [One Emoji, One Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-emoji-one-smile-5470751f) bounty on make.fun.

## The emoji

> 🤗

A hugging face: open arms, warm and friendly at first glance, readable by a stranger of any age or background, and clearly chosen rather than the first smiley that comes to mind. No intimacy, no sarcasm, no in-joke — just a virtual hug from someone unknown.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the emoji to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically (two runs produce byte-identical output).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
